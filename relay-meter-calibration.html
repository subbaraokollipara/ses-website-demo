Relay & Meter Calibration

Testing and calibration of protection relays and electrical meters.

Overview

Relay & Meter Calibration

Protection relays and meters must respond accurately for a system to be safe and correctly measured. SES tests and calibrates relays and meters using dedicated relay testing and energy-meter testing kits.

Relays & meters covered

  • Over-current relays
  • Earth-fault relays
  • UV & OV (under/over-voltage) relays
  • Auxiliary relays
  • kWh meters
  • Ammeters
  • Voltmeters
Enquire Now Call Us

Typical use cases

  • Periodic relay testing for protection reliability
  • Verifying meter accuracy
  • After relay replacement or setting changes
  • Before commissioning a new panel

Information that helps us quote

  • Relay / meter make, model and quantity
  • Existing relay settings, if available
  • Site location
  • Preferred dates
