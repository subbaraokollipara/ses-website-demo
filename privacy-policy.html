Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us through this website.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Santosh Electrical Services (SES), Plot No. 85, Jayanagar, Opp. KPHB, Kukatpally, Hyderabad – 500072, Telangana, India.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we collect the details you provide: your name, company, email address, phone number, enquiry type, location, message, any panel details and any file you choose to attach. We do not ask for sensitive personal data and request that you do not include it.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and communicate with you about the services or products you asked about. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How it is handled

Form submissions are sent by email to SES staff who handle enquiries. Attachments are emailed and are not published on this website. Access is limited to people who need it to respond to you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and for our ordinary business records, after which it is deleted.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If we introduce website analytics, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first. Map links open Google Maps, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, by writing to ssantoshelectrical@gmail.com or calling 040-23066178.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
