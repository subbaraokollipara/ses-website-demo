Company Overview

Santosh Electrical Services

Santosh Electrical Services (SES) provides electrical sales, servicing and manufacturing of panels, with over 20 years of experience in the electrical industry across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

We were established with a clear vision — to deliver high-quality products and services to all our customers with integrity and professionalism.

We specialise in servicing, AMC, commissioning, testing, retrofitting, installation and upgradation of LV and MV switchgear, panels, relays and metering solutions. Our solutions are designed to ensure safety, reliability and optimum performance for industrial, commercial and infrastructure applications.
