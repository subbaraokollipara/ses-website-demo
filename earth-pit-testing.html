Overview

Earth Pit Testing

A healthy earthing system is essential for the safety of people and equipment. SES tests earth pits with earth testers, maintains existing pits and installs new earth pits.

Earth pit services

  • Earth-pit testing
  • Earth-pit maintenance
  • New earth-pit installation
Enquire Now Call Us

Typical use cases

  • Periodic earth-resistance checks
  • New buildings, panels or equipment requiring earthing
  • Earth pits showing high resistance
  • Pre-commissioning checks

Information that helps us quote

  • Number of earth pits and their locations
  • Site type and location
  • Previous test readings, if any
  • Whether new pits are required
