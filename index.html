About SES

Reliable Electrical Solutions with Experience & Commitment

Santosh Electrical Services provides electrical sales, servicing and manufacturing of panels, with over 20 years of experience in the electrical industry across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

We specialise in servicing, AMC, commissioning, testing, retrofitting, installation and upgradation of LV and MV switchgear, panels, relays and metering solutions — designed for safety, reliability and optimum performance in industrial, commercial and infrastructure applications.
